# Dashell RSS 2.7.5

## Find articles and recover quickly

Search article titles across every page in the current view, regardless of case or surrounding spaces. Result counts stay in sync, and Clear search returns you to your articles. Empty dashboards now offer Add your first feed directly.

## Clearer subscription feedback

See when a subscription is being added, keep your inputs after a failure, and retry without filling the form again. Closing a pending save clearly explains that adding continues in the background. Chinese title composition no longer triggers form shortcuts.

## Easier navigation and reading

Use Enter or Space to toggle the sidebar with a visible focus indicator. Closing sidebar search restores the full feed list. Narrow screens keep compact cover actions and larger touch targets; empty results stay readable across the full card grid.

This update also includes inherited tag colors, cancellable page cover fetching, article title scaling, and improvements to saved Markdown tables, images and filenames. Existing subscriptions and learning materials remain available.
