# Dashell RSS 2.7.2

Keep your RSS subscriptions in Dashell RSS and continue reading text articles
in Dashell Reader.

## Open articles in Dashell Reader

Open an article card to save its available text as a local Markdown note and
read it in Dashell Reader. Reopening an article reuses the saved file, and
supplied original, translation, and rewrite versions remain available for
switching. Article links are preserved as text for Reader lookup.

On first launch, Dashell RSS copies existing RSS Dashboard settings, feed
shards, article state, and learning records into its own plugin folder. The
original data remains available for rollback.

## Keep media previews in Dashell RSS

Podcast and video cards keep the existing RSS preview and player. They do not require an automatic download or Dashell Player.

## Also improved

- Settings tabs are localized in Simplified Chinese.
- Feed, article, and storage behavior includes the upstream fixes accumulated
  since RSS Dashboard 2.7.1.
