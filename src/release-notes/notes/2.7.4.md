# Dashell RSS 2.7.4

## Find sources before saving

Preview feeds and discover the subscriptions declared by a website. Choose the source you want, or open OPML in the existing import selection. Cancelled previews and repeated saves no longer leave duplicate subscriptions.

## Move through longer articles

Use the reading outline to jump between headings and follow your current section. The outline updates when you switch between original, translated and rewritten versions. Reading settings are directly available in the workbench toolbar.

## More reliable reading and exports

Long lists retain their position and support Chinese input. Cached content appears first, requests share the existing network budget, and settings saves run in order. WeChat original links and malformed rewrite images are handled safely, including images saved for Dashell Reader.

Your existing feeds, learning files and per-version progress remain in place. Update Dashell Reader and Player separately when you use their integrations.
