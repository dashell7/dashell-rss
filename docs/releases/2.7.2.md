# Dashell RSS 2.7.2

Dashell RSS keeps the RSS Dashboard subscription tree and article dashboard,
with a direct reading handoff to Dashell Reader for text articles.

## Read text articles in Dashell Reader

Selecting an article card prepares its available content as a local Markdown
file and opens that file in Dashell Reader. Reopening a saved article reuses the
file, preserving notes and the Reader's saved progress. When a source supplies
original, translated, or rewritten versions, the Reader can switch among the
versions that are present.

Saved article links are converted to text so Reader's hover and click lookup can
work on linked words. Images and source URLs are retained. Settings, feed
shards, article state, and learning records from RSS Dashboard are copied to
Dashell RSS on first launch; the original plugin data stays in place for
rollback.

## Keep podcasts and videos in RSS previews

Podcast and video cards continue to use RSS Dashboard's existing preview and media players. They are not automatically downloaded, and opening them does not require Dashell Player.

## Additional improvements

This release also includes RSS Dashboard changes accumulated after 2.7.1,
including improvements to keyboard and touch access, article and feed
management, podcast playback, Reader readability, and storage reliability. The
detailed changes are listed in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).
